Thank you for installing the Enterprise Inference Helm Chart!

To access your application, follow these steps:

1. Check the status of your deployment:
    kubectl get all -n <namespace>

2. Retrieve the service URL:
    kubectl get svc -n <namespace>

3. If your service is of type LoadBalancer, wait for the external IP to be assigned:
    kubectl get svc -w -n <namespace>

4. If your service is of type NodePort, use the following command to get the NodePort:
    kubectl get svc -n <namespace> -o jsonpath='{.spec.ports[0].nodePort}'

5. Access the application using the appropriate URL:
    - LoadBalancer: http://<external-ip>:<port>
    - NodePort: http://<node-ip>:<node-port>

For more information, refer to the documentation or contact your administrator.
